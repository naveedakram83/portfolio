Why this work matters in the United States

As US payments move to real time, the middleware layer carries more of the risk.

REAL-TIME PAYMENTS

Less time to stop fraud.

The Federal Reserve's FedNow Service settles payments in seconds, around the clock. When money moves that fast, fraud checks have to run inside the message path. That is the kind of layer I have built for banks in the Middle East and Asia.

SHARED STANDARDS

The same building blocks.

US ATM and POS networks run on the standards I deliver every day: ISO 8583 messaging, EMV chip cards and PCI DSS. The lessons carry over directly.

CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Payments are critical infrastructure.

CISA lists financial services among the 16 critical infrastructure sectors. I treat payment middleware the same way, and I am completing CISA training in industrial control systems security.

Academic research

MSc Artificial Intelligence dissertation · University of West London

Adaptive Traffic Signal Control Using Reinforcement Learning Across Multiple Traffic Scenarios

A multi-scenario evaluation of DQN, DDQN and PPO signal control in SUMO, showing how baseline strength, training variability and metric choice change the conclusions.

  • Primary DQN agent trained on medium demand, then tested without retraining on low, medium, high, rush-hour and uneven demand
  • Extensions: DDQN, PPO, a two-intersection corridor and emergency-vehicle-aware control
  • 8 scenario configurations in the SUMO traffic simulator
